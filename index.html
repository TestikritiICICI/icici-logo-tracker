The check runs every day at 9:00 AM IST on GitHub's servers. When the Mac in India is on, it then checks every brand again (Indian sites load more reliably from India, and its text recognition reads logos inside banners better) and its results replace the server's for that day.

How it works: each brand's homepage is opened in Google Chrome, scrolled, and checked for "ICICI" in image names and alt text, in page text, and inside images using text recognition. "Other banks only" means other banks appear but ICICI does not. Checkout pages and mobile apps are not covered.
